SYNTHETIC TRAINING EXAMPLE — NOT AN APPROVED SOP
Document ID: DEMO-QA-001 | Version: 1.0 | Department: Quality

Purpose
This fictional example demonstrates how a quality event may be documented in a training environment.

Recording a deviation
The example record includes an event identifier, date and time observed, process or area, concise factual description, immediate containment action if applicable, reporter, and reference to supporting evidence.

Review
A designated quality reviewer evaluates the record for completeness. This training text does not define real company requirements, regulatory obligations, or product disposition decisions.

Traceability
Changes to this example record should be documented with the editor, timestamp, and reason for change.
